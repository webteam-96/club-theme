New club website theme: design samples

Seven directions for the new scroll-animated theme. Every sample is built with Rotary Club of Thane Hills' real content: banners, projects, leaders, newsletters and meeting details.

Open a sample and scroll slowly: the animation follows your scroll. They look best on a laptop in Chrome, and each one also works on a phone. Links inside the samples are switched off.
